# 0002: One window; work on files in windows of their own

Accepted, 25 November 2026.

As Kalibre (its 0002): the music is in one window. Editing tags, converting, renaming, ripping, Preferences and the add-on list are windows of their own, as Kalibre's settings windows are, because they are work on files beside the music, and close with Escape. Small questions (a name, a pattern) are asked over the window with ui::present.
