# 0013: DSP: equaliser, then plug-ins, then volume

Accepted, 25 November 2026.

On the audio thread, after decoding: a ten-band peaking equaliser an octave apart (31 Hz to 16 kHz, +-12 dB) with a preamp, then AU and VST3 effects in a chain, then the volume. ReplayGain is applied earlier, per track, in the decoding thread, so a gain belongs to its track exactly. The chain is rebuilt and swapped whole on any change; plug-in settings are captured first and kept in Settings.xml.
